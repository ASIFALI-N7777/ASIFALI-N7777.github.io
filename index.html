Sales Data Analysis Pipeline

ExcelPower BI / DAX PythonPowerShellSQL

An end-to-end pipeline that turns a deliberately dirty 10,000+ row sales extract into an auditable, refreshable KPI dashboard.

Defect in the raw extractHow it was handled
Duplicate postingsMATCH first-occurrence test drives a duplicate flag
Three date formatsDATE / DATEVALUE / SEARCH — locale-independent parsing
Currency stored as textSUBSTITUTE + VALUE conversion to number
Blank prices, missing categoriesXLOOKUP against the product master
Casing, padding, region aliasesTRIM / UPPER plus an alias-to-canonical mapping
Cancelled & returned rowsNested IF inclusion rules with a stated exclusion reason
  • Auditable by design. Every column of the clean layer is a live Excel formula reading from the raw sheet — nothing pre-computed in Python, so a reviewer can click any cell and see why a row was kept, corrected or dropped.
  • Stable measure layer. A deterministic SUMIFS/AVERAGEIFS summary sits between pivots and charts, so visuals do not break when a pivot field expands.
  • Dashboard. DAX measures for Total Sales, Profit % and YoY Growth, five PivotTables on a shared cache, three slicers, and a loss watchlist showing where margin is leaking.
  • Reproducible. Three scripts rebuild the whole thing from the raw extract — the monthly refresh is re-run, not rebuilt.

Cognitive Drift Detection System

Published — IJRTI 2026
Machine LearningPython Learning Analytics

A web-based system that flags student cognitive drift early enough for a teacher to act — final year project, published as a peer-reviewed paper.

  • The gap it closes. Decline is normally noticed at assessment time. The behavioural signals precede it by weeks, but nobody is watching them.
  • Features. Session frequency, time-on-task, assessment trend, response-time patterns and interaction depth over a rolling window.
  • Models. Logistic Regression and Random Forest compared under 5-fold cross-validation with hyperparameter tuning; classified into Normal, Early Drift and High Drift.
  • Measured on the right thing. Precision, recall and F1 rather than raw accuracy — missing a drifting student costs more than a false alarm, so recall was protected deliberately.
  • Teacher-facing. Dashboards surface who crossed a threshold this week, so the output reads as a name to check on, not a model score.

Smart Ration Distribution System

First Prize — National 2025
IoTAutomation Public Systems

A digitised public distribution system that makes ration release traceable and hard to defraud.

  • Identity-gated release. Ration is dispensed only after the beneficiary's unique ID card is scanned — removing the discretionary step where most leakage occurred.
  • Immutable log. Every dispense records who, what, when and which shop; duplicate claims fail because the entitlement is already marked for that period.
  • IoT integration. Barcode/RFID hardware writes to a central database, giving live stock and dispatch visibility instead of monthly reconciliation.
  • Result. Fraudulent and duplicate collection blocked at the point of dispense, with a full audit trail for oversight.
